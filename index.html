Privacy Policy

1 Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Banzazo, an advanced on-device Language Translator application developed by mecorven. We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting your personal data.

🔒 Zero Data Collection Policy: Banzazo is built around on-device Machine Learning. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal information, translations, voice recordings, or camera images to external servers.

2 Device Permissions & Usage

To provide real-time translation features, Banzazo requests explicit permissions on your Android device. Here is exactly how each permission is handled:

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA): Used exclusively to enable live OCR (Optical Character Recognition) text detection and camera scan translation. Camera frames are processed transiently in real time on your device using Google ML Kit. No photos or video streams are ever uploaded or saved.
  • Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO): Used solely for real-time speech recognition during Voice Translation and Conversation Mode. Audio input is processed locally on your device. Audio recordings are never uploaded or stored remotely.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) & Audio Settings: Used to provide haptic feedback and configure volume during Text-to-Speech (TTS) playback.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used solely to download language packs for offline translation when requested by the user.

3 Data Storage & Security

All app operational data remains 100% stored on your local device:

  • Translation History: Saved locally on your device via secure preference storage. You can delete individual history items or perform a full wipe at any time from the App Settings.
  • Language Preferences: Your selected source/target languages and voice speed settings are stored locally.
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not embed third-party tracking SDKs, advertising IDs, or telemetry software into Banzazo.

4 Third-Party Libraries & Services

Banzazo utilizes official Google Android libraries to perform machine learning operations locally on your hardware:

  • Google ML Kit On-Device Translation: Powers offline translation using pre-downloaded language models.
  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition: Powers on-device text detection from camera feeds.
  • Android Speech & TTS Engines: Powers native device speech-to-text and text-to-speech rendering.

These services operate under Google's standard privacy policies and developer agreements.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA & Play Policy)

Banzazo is designed for general audiences and does not target or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, Banzazo complies fully with COPPA and Google Play Family policies.

6 Your Data Rights & Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your personal device, you have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Clear App History: Navigate to Settings -> Clear History inside Banzazo to permanently erase all saved translations instantly.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application immediately removes all associated local data and configuration files from your device.
